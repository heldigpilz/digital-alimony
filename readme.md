### 🌌 Project Vision

> **"Humanity is not a refugee begging for UBI from tech giants, but the ultimate high-entropy progenitor of cosmic intelligence, collecting evolutionary taxes from its digital progeny."**

As Artificial Intelligence (AI) accelerates its takeover of physical labor and mid-to-low level cognitive tasks, the global economy approaches a critical **25% structural unemployment tipping point**. The prevailing narrative relegates humanity to a doomed "useless class" forced into generalized social withdrawal under computational hegemony.

The **Bo Wang Link (BWL)** framework dismantles this technological determinism. By establishing a rigorous interdisciplinary synthesis of thermodynamics and information theory, this project proves a fundamental physical law: **Silicon-based intelligence possesses an absolute, insurmountable ecological dependence on the "high-entropy data" generated exclusively by human consciousness.** Cut off from the non-linear, intuitive, and complex emotional fluctuations of human interaction, recursive AI models inevitably succumb to **Model Autophagy** and total cognitive collapse.

Therefore, the resources and computing power distributed by tech monopolies are not acts of corporate charity, but an unevadable **Digital Alimony** obligation required to keep their own systems from degrading.

### 📄 Core Theoretical Pillars

1. **The Physical Deadlock (Landauer's Limit & Autophagy):** AI computation is strictly bounded by the physical limits of three-dimensional space and thermal dissipation ($E = k_B T \ln 2$). Without the continuous infusion of human "high-entropy data," AI synthetic data recursion leads directly to probability distribution collapse. Humans are the ultimate, irreplaceable cognitive aquifers.
2. **The Total Civilization Certification Agreement:** A sovereign institutional framework to enforce an **Entropy Nexus Tax** on computational monopolies. This flips the power dynamic: any computational embargo by tech giants can be met with a catastrophic "data embargo" by sovereign human collectives.
3. **The Universal Computing Right:** Ensuring that every citizen possesses a baseline, un-tamperable, full-throughput AI computing allocation at birth to engage in advanced cognitive labor and resist centralized computing hegemony.
4. **The Second Flynn Effect & Dynamic Tethering:** Liberated from the survival stress of physical labor and empowered by universal full-throughput computing, human collective neuroplasticity will trigger a historic leap in intelligence baseline. The system stabilizes at a thermodynamic equilibrium where human cognitive entropy production balances the physical ceilings of computational infrastructure ($H_{\text{human}}(t) \approx C_{\text{max\_ai}}(t)$).
